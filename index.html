The Story So Far

Caden Albright is a singer-songwriter based in The Woodlands, Texas, who draws from his own experiences to create songs that have real meaning to him. Caden has a long-standing background in music, having played the guitar for a decade, the piano for a decade, and singing from a young age.

Caden developed his passion for music under the mentorship of two-time Grammy-nominated artist Sarah Kelly and producer Jonas Ekman, leading to Caden recording and releasing his debut single "Love Turned Cold" in July 2025.

"Love Turned Cold" was recorded in Nashville, Tennessee, with recording engineering done by Jeremy Hunter and produced by Sweekman.

Following its release, "Love Turned Cold" earned a spot on the Grammy ballot. Caden followed it with his second single, "Painless," in June 2026, and in the summer of 2026 he recorded his upcoming single, "Lies," in Los Angeles with producer Mike Clink.

Beyond the studio, Caden performs regularly around Houston, playing paid gigs and headline sets at venues like Dosey Doe and Market Street.

For Caden, music is also a way to give back. He teaches guitar and piano, and he founded The Harmony Project, a volunteer organization that brings teen musicians to perform for hospital patients. He is currently a freshman at Stanford University with interests in health tech and music technology.
